How it is measured

Who has the ball
Your tagged passes, plus the player nearest the ball on screen to time tackles and loose balls between passes.
Thirds
Pitch positions from the camera calibration, fitted line by line to the real pitch (goal box, penalty box, centre circle, halfway line), smoothed over half a second and read at each kick and touch. Each team's attacking direction comes from where its goalkeeper plays, per half.
Quick first action
Within 5 s of winning the ball: a pass gaining 10 m or more toward goal (within 45° of straight), or the ball carried 10 m forward.
Fast advance
At least 2.5 m/s toward goal over the first 12 s.
Exposed opponent
At the moment the ball is won, 5 or fewer of the opponent's outfield players on screen are between the ball and their goal (only when 6 or more are visible).
Where it ended
The furthest third reached in the first 12 s, before the ball was lost.
