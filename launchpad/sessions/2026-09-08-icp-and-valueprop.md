## Where it will get challenged — fix these tonight

### 1. Three customers is not an ICP
The plan targets pro clubs, academies, *and* federations. GTM sequences them, but the ICP
must be **one**: the Phase-1 beachhead — **private elite football academies in the GCC that
sell players.** Be able to say out loud: "not pro clubs yet, not federations yet."

### 2. "Mid-market academy" is a segment, not an ICP
An ICP is a filter you can apply to a name and get yes/no. Draft:
- Private academy, GCC, 150–600 registered players
- **Sells or transfers players** ← the sharpest qualifier; without this the Verified Asset Ledger has no buyer
- Already films matches (any camera) but does nothing structured with the footage
- 0–1 analysts on staff — enough pain to feel it, not enough budget to solve it
- Owner or technical director controls a discretionary budget ≥100k SAR
- Under pressure to look professional to parents / federation / sponsors

Bring this to the session and ask the speaker to attack it.

### 3. Buyer ≠ user (the point of your own Q today)
| Role | Person | Their job |
|---|---|---|
| **Economic buyer** | Owner / technical director | Prove the academy's value; transfer income; reputation |
| **User** | Head of performance / analyst | Produce insight without a data team |
| **User / champion** | Coach | Decide selection & tactics without guessing |
| **Blocker** | Whoever owns the current spreadsheet workflow | Doesn't want to look replaceable |

The Verified Asset Ledger sells the **buyer**. The tactical dashboard sells the **user**.
Two different value propositions for one product — say so explicitly; it shows sophistication.

## Value Proposition session — the trap to avoid
The PDD leads with features: xT, Pressure Resistance, pass networks, KEMO. Sherzod ran the
JTBD masterclass; he will push for **jobs and outcomes, not features.**

Reframe as jobs:
| Job (customer's words) | Type | What you deliver |
|---|---|---|
| "Prove this player is worth what I'm asking" | functional + economic | Verified Asset Ledger |
| "Stop deciding from gut feeling" | functional | Automated team-level tactical metrics |
| "Compete with clubs that have a data department" | social | Elite metrics, no data team, no hardware |
| "Understand the numbers in my own language" | emotional | KEMO, Arabic-native |

**Draft one-liner to test tonight:**
> For GCC academies that develop and sell players but can't afford a data department,
> Playbook-IQ turns ordinary match video into elite tactical metrics and a verified record
> of each player's progression — no cameras, no hardware, no analysts to hire.

Note what's absent: xT, homography, computer vision. Nobody buys those.

### Two-tier framing (settled 8 Sep)
Every team gets **automatic analysis metrics** in the base tier; teams wanting a specific
custom measurement upgrade to **premium**, where Playbook-IQ handles the science and
contacts the suitable lab — **the club never deals with the lab.** That brokered layer is
the moat: state it plainly in the value prop.

### Consequences — apply these

**1. Primary ICP is the buyer.** The academy **owner / technical director** who controls the

The dashboard answers the user's job. Buyer-first means the Ledger moves to the front of
the pitch, and the dashboard becomes what keeps the academy using it daily.
