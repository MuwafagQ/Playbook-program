## Session 2 — Ministry of Sport SportTech Challenge Deep-Dive (6:40pm) ⭐
**The most important hour of your week.** This is your track sponsor, your Ask #3, and the
owner of the footage you're currently using without licence.

### The one thing to get out of it: the footage question
Ask it as a partnership question, not a confession:

> "Our system runs entirely on broadcast footage — no cameras, no hardware. For a startup in
> this challenge, what's the sanctioned path to licensed access to league footage? Who should
> I be speaking to?"

Framed that way it's the most natural question a sports-tech founder could ask, it signals
you've built something real, and it opens a door instead of creating a problem. **Do not
mention how you obtained your current footage.**

### Also get
- **What the challenge actually rewards.** Scoring criteria, what "winning" the track means,
  whether there's procurement or a pilot attached. Write it down — this shapes Assignment #2.
- **Names.** Who at the Ministry owns sports data and digital transformation? Ask how to reach
  them. Your PDD Ask #3 is exactly this.
- **The governance mandate — get the facts.** You believe the Ministry funds academies and
  requires advanced analytics, with compliance rewarded. **Verify it.** Which academies, what
  exactly is required, is there a deadline, who audits it. This is a core assumption in your
  business model and you're currently working from hearsay.
- **Introductions to academies.** They know every academy in the Kingdom. If there's a natural
  moment, ask.

### Do
- Turn your camera on. Speak. Be memorable — you're one of 250 and this is a small track.
- Say who you are in one line: *"Muwafag, Playbook-IQ — we turn broadcast footage into
  tactical analysis for academies, no hardware."*
- Follow up in writing the same day to anyone who speaks. Ask one specific question.

### Don't
- Pitch. It's an information session, not a demo day.
- Ask anything you could have found in the challenge brief.
