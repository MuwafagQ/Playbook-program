## The answer is already drafted — see `../TECH_REALITY.md`
The word **"realistic"** in the brief is the whole assignment. This is where the honest
technical position becomes an advantage rather than an exposure.

**Proposed structure:**

**Tier 1 — automated, self-serve (Phase 2 MVP).** Team-level tactical intelligence computed
from detection + team classification + homography alone, with **no Re-ID dependency**:
territory and occupation heatmaps, team centroid, block height, width, compactness,
defensive line height, pressing intensity by zone, formation shape by match phase, pitch
control, phase segmentation. All of this runs unattended on the existing pipeline today.

**Tier 2 — lab, analyst-assisted (Phase 3+).** Individual player metrics, xT contribution,
Pressure Resistance, individual pass networks, the Verified Asset Ledger. Human in the loop
by design — this is the "Fractional Sports Science Lab" pillar and the "Custom Metric
Library" upsell already in the business model.

**Tier 3 — KEMO, the Arabic-native AI analyst.** Decide deliberately whether this is Phase 2
or Phase 3. It is a large build and it is not what wins a first pilot; but it is the most
memorable thing in the pitch. *Open question for Muwafag.*

**The roadmap narrative:** the automation ratio between Tier 1 and Tier 2 increases over time.
That is the technical roadmap, it is measurable, and it is honest.

## Why this scores well
Most founders present a vision the technology can't support. Presenting a sequenced plan
that names exactly what is automated today, what needs a human, and what the roadmap
converts from the second to the first — with the pipeline's own confidence flags as evidence
— reads as engineering maturity. That is rare at Phase 1 and it is what survives Phase 4
diligence.

## Prerequisite
Run the pipeline unattended on one full half and measure the automation ratio (see
`../TECH_REALITY.md` priority 3). Real numbers turn this from an argument into a finding.
