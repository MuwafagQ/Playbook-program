## Interview discipline (JTBD)
- Ask about the **last time** they analysed a match, not what they'd like.
- What do they do **today**, and what does it cost in hours and money?
- **Never pitch.** Describing the product contaminates the interview.
- "What did you try before, and why did you stop?"
- For the Verified Asset Ledger: *"Walk me through the last player you sold. How did you
  justify the price?"*
- Record (with permission) — "proof" is in the assignment title; you likely need evidence.
