## Interview discipline (JTBD — do not skip)
- Ask about the **last time** they analysed a match, not what they'd "like."
- Ask what they do **today** and what it costs them in hours and money.
- Never pitch. If you describe the product, the interview is contaminated.
- Ask "what did you try before, and why did you stop?"
- The killer question for the Verified Asset Ledger: *"Walk me through the last player you
  sold. How did you justify the price?"*
