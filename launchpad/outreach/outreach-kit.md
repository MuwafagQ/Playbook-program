## 6. Discipline
- **Send 25–30 to land 4.** Volume, today.
- Track every send in a simple sheet: name, role, channel, date sent, date followed up, outcome.
- **Follow up once after 3 days.** One line: "Just floating this back up — still glad to have
  20 minutes if you can spare it."
- Offer specific times, not "when suits you." Two options converts better than an open question.
- Never attach the deck. It converts a research request into a sales pitch.
