## Prioritise triggered buyers (Darrena, slide 6)
Triggered buyers close 3–5× faster. **Our trigger is the Ministry governance mandate.**

Prioritise anyone who has recently:
- Posted about **Ministry of Sport funding, licensing, or club privatisation**
- Announced a **digital transformation or performance initiative**
- **Started the role in the last 12 months** — new directors buy; settled ones don't
- Posted about **selling or transferring a player**
- Posted about **hiring an analyst** — they have the pain and are trying to solve it the
  expensive way. Job postings are the strongest single signal on LinkedIn.

Set a saved search and check it weekly. Also follow the **Ministry of Sport** and the **Saudi
Arabian Football Federation** pages and read who comments.

## Discipline
- **30–40 connection requests to land 3 interviews.** Directors accept at a low rate.
- Offer two specific times, not "when suits you."
- Follow up **once** after 3 days. Then stop.
- **Never attach the deck.** It turns research into a sales pitch and kills the response rate.
- Track every one: name, academy, title, date sent, accepted, replied, outcome.
