whole youth thesis points at the right data layer.

### §1 — His academy years (before the demo)

1. *"What did your staff open every week? What did nobody look at?"*
2. *"What did you have to do by hand that you wished was automatic?"*
3. *"You built growth & maturation reporting at Olympiacos too. Is that what academies genuinely
   act on — more than tactics?"*
4. *"Who decided what tools the academy got — you, the academy director, or the club above?"*
5. *"What did a coach actually change after reading one of your reports?"* — this is the question
   we could not answer for our own product.

### §2 — Metric critique (after the demo)

1. *"Are these defensible? Would you have let staff make decisions on them?"*
2. *"Which of these would you drop, and which is missing?"*
3. *"Lizzie described a coach who used total distance as his main metric and it changed how
   players ran. Do you see that failure mode in what I've built?"*
4. *"Does Catapult extract tactical data from broadcast footage, or does it need your own
   cameras?"* — the answer tells us whether Catapult can structurally reach grassroots at all.

### §3 — Market and money ⭐

**He is a vendor, not a customer.** The Mom Test rule against hypothetical pricing does not apply
here — he has real numbers from three companies. Ask directly.

1. *"What does an academy-level deal actually cost — STATSports, Kitman, Catapult? Ballpark."*
2. *"Does an academy hold its own budget, or does everything come from the club?"*
3. *"Where do these deals die — price, or priority?"*
4. *"Annual, per season, per team, per athlete — how is it usually structured?"*
5. *"At Kitman Labs you had Al-Ittihad. How did Saudi actually buy? Who signed, how long did it
   take, and what nearly killed it?"*
6. *"What makes a club renew — and what makes them quietly not?"*

### §4 — Close

1. *"Would you be willing to look at this again once I've run it on a few real matches?"*
2. *"Is there anyone in Saudi or the region I should be speaking to?"*
