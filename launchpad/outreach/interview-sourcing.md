introduce me to three people?"* Referrals from these two are the single highest-conversion

Why this works: names the credential, names the accelerator (institutional legitimacy —

motive, asks for a small bounded slot, and offers something back.

**Volume rule:** send 25–30 to land 4. Send them in the next 48 hours — response lag is the
real risk, not rejection.

## Discipline in the room
- Ask about the **last time**, never the hypothetical.
- What do they do today? What does it cost in hours and money?
- "What did you try before, and why did you stop?"
- **Do not pitch.** If you describe the product, the interview is contaminated.
- For the Verified Asset Ledger: *"Walk me through the last player you sold. How did you
  justify the price?"*
- Ask permission to record — the assignment wants *proof*.
- Close every one with: *"Who else should I talk to?"*
