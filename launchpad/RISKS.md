# Open risks — internal, candid

## 1. Footage provenance (HIGH — act this week)
Match footage came from the **official Ministry of Sport platform, obtained informally
through friends, without legal access rights.**

Why this is urgent rather than merely awkward:
- The **Ministry of Sport is a program sponsor and the owner of the SportTech Challenge
  track this venture sits in.** They will be attached to the cohort.
- Ask #3 in the PDD is explicitly *"establish a connection with the Ministry of Sport."*
- Data rights are the first thing any sports-tech investor diligences, because the entire
  business model depends on lawful access to broadcast footage.

**This is not only a risk — it is the single best opportunity in Week 1.**
The **Ministry of Sport SportTech Challenge Deep-Dive is Wed 9 Sep 6:40pm.** The correct
move is to arrive with a legitimate request: *"our pipeline runs on broadcast footage;
what is the sanctioned path to licensed access to league footage for an accredited
Launchpad startup?"* Asked that way it is a partnership question, not a confession.

Until access is licensed:
- Do **not** publish, distribute, or demo the raw source video externally.
- Derived outputs (tracking coordinates, annotated stills for a pitch) are far lower risk
  than redistributing the footage itself, but this is not legal advice — Ask #2
  (regulatory support) should cover it. Raise it in a 1:1.
- Do not commit source match video to this repo.

## 2. Deck-vs-reality gap (MEDIUM — manage before Phase 4)
The PDD presents xT, pass networks, and Pressure Resistance as instant and out-of-the-box,
and the mapping as automatic. Today passes are hand-tagged and identities are hand-unified.
See `TECH_REALITY.md`. Mentors in Phase 1 will not catch this. Technical due diligence at
Phase 4 will. Reframe now, on your own terms, while it reads as rigour rather than as a
correction.

## 3. Solo founder (MEDIUM — structural)
250 → 125 → 34. Solo technical founders with no commercial co-founder are a known
filtration risk, and the honest gap Muwafag already names is a CV expert. Ask #1 (CV lab
access) partly addresses this. Worth deciding early whether the program is a route to a
technical co-founder, not only to a lab.

## 4. Customer evidence (HIGH — this is Assignment #1)
Two youth-league experts liked the product. That is warmth, not evidence. There is no
signed pilot, no LOI, no paying customer, and the stated struggle is **getting more
conversations.** Everything in Phase 1 filtration will key off this. See `assignments/`.

## 5. Market sizing will be challenged (LOW–MEDIUM)
SAM is given as *the Saudi sports sector* (84B SAR) rather than the serviceable
*analytics* slice of it. The Competitive Analysis and Understanding Your Market
masterclasses (Mon 14 Sep) are where this gets probed. SOM (600 academies × 100k SAR) is
the credible number and is well constructed — consider leading with it and deriving SAM
more narrowly.
