## Problem (per PDD)
1. Coaching decisions are intuition-driven; existing tools are rigid and one-size-fits-all.
2. 75% of Tier-1 clubs use advanced data; ~90% of mid-market academies are priced out
   (in-house lab ≈ $300k/yr).
3. Academies can't mathematically prove player progression → lose transfer value.

## Solution — three pillars
- **Philosophy-to-Metric Engine** — translate a club's tactical philosophy into custom automated KPIs.
- **Fractional Sports Science Labs** — managed gateway to lab-grade metrics at a fraction of cost.
- **The Verified Asset Ledger** — continuous mathematical proof of player progression for transfer valuation.

## Product
- Tactical video mapping (upload broadcast footage → tracking synced to match phases:
  build-up, transition, finishing)
- Built-in metrics: xT, pass networks, Pressure Resistance (PR)
- **KEMO** — conversational AI analyst, Arabic + English, interprets the graphs

## Market (as pitched)
- TAM 54.3B SAR (global sports analytics by 2030)
- SAM 84B SAR (Saudi sports sector by 2030, Vision 2030 / PIF-SURJ driven)
- SOM 60M SAR (~600 private elite academies + newly privatized mid-market clubs @ 100k SAR ACV)

## Business model
1. Tiered annual B2B SaaS (core platform + dashboards + KEMO)
2. Custom metric library — high-margin upsell, exclusive to the buyer's dashboard
3. Margin advantage — orchestrate cost-efficient rates with global academic sports labs,
   package their output as sticky enterprise software

## GTM
- **Phase 1 Beachhead** — direct to private elite academies + newly privatized lower-tier GCC clubs; zero hardware, zero infrastructure onboarding.
- **Phase 2 Trojan Horse** — academy sells a player, hands the Playbook-IQ ledger to the buying Tier-1 club as proof of value → buyer must engage the platform → zero-CAC enterprise adoption.
- **Phase 3 Federation alignment** — become the official digital standard for regional federations digitizing under Vision 2030.

## Competitive position
Claimed quadrant: **High tactical intelligence + low infrastructure requirement.**
- Hudl / Stats Perform — Tier-1 Western budgets, need in-house data scientists, no regional benchmarks, no Arabic-native AI layer.
- Catapult / GPS wearables — physical workload only, no tactical/decision intelligence.
Differentiators Muwafag names: business model, localization, customization, and operating
**as a science lab, not a solution provider.**
