## The two rules
- **Never pitch.** If you describe the product, the interview is contaminated.
- **Ask about the past, not the future.** "What did you do last time?" not "would you use this?"

## Volume reality

replies take 2–4 days. It is 9 Sep. **Everything must be sent within 48 hours.**
