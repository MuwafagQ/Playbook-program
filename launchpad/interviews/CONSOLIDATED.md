   analysts."
2. **Time is the user's pain; evidence is the buyer's.** Two different value propositions from
   one product.
3. **Nobody can evaluate advanced metrics** — the user cannot assess the differentiator, which
   is why conclusions must be delivered rather than dashboards.

   come again." Stated trust tests: adaptability, sustainability, education.

   injuries. Nothing about what happened on the pitch.
