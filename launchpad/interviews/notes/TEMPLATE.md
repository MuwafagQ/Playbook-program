## 6. The three jobs
`[→ JTBD canvas: functional / emotional / social]`
- **Functional** — what do they want to get done?
- **Emotional** — how do they want to feel? (confident, not blamed, in control)
- **Social** — how do they want to be seen? (by the board, parents, federation, other clubs)

*B2B buyers have all three. Career risk, fear of blame, and wanting to look forward-thinking
are real and rarely stated directly — infer them from tone as well as words.*
