# Team onboarding — start here
For the Data Operations Specialist joining Playbook-IQ for the MISK Launchpad program.

Welcome. This file tells you what the company is, what the program is, what your role is,
and what to do first. Read it fully before anything else.

## What Playbook-IQ is
A **science lab and data department for mid-league football teams.** We take ordinary
broadcast match footage — no cameras, no hardware installed at the ground — and turn it into
curated tactical metrics and live in-match notifications.

The founder is **Muwafag Hussain**: football performance analyst, tactical analysis diploma
from Barça Innovation Hub, background in GIS, data and computer vision.

**Our conviction:** most analytics platforms drown clubs in numbers. Our vision is
*separating the needed metrics from the noise.* Curation is the product.

**Where we are:** working prototype at `app.playbookiq.ai`. One full match processed
end-to-end. Two youth-league experts have seen it and liked it. **No paying customers yet.**

## What the program is
MISK Launchpad Cohort 10, on the **Ministry of Sport SportTech track**. Four phases from
ideation to investment readiness, running 7 Sep 2026 – 2 Jan 2027, with filtration rounds
between them: **250 startups → 125 → 34.**

Read `PROGRAM.md` for the full timeline and `OBLIGATIONS.md` for the deadlines that cannot
be missed.

## Your role
**Data Operations Specialist.** Full description in `ROLE-data-operations-engineer.md`.

Short version — three things:
1. **Match data quality** — annotating passes and tactical events, correcting player
   identities, reviewing what the system got wrong.
2. **Quality measurement** — tracking how well the system performs unattended. The headline
   number is **human hours spent per match.** That figure is the single most important metric
   in the company and nobody owns it yet. It becomes yours.
3. **Research and operations** — building the academy and agency contact list, competitor and
   pricing research, interview scheduling and notes.

**No programming required.** What the work needs is systematic thinking, comfort with
structured data, and patience with detail.

## Your first week
1. Read the above.
2. Watch one 10-minute clip alongside the system's output. Write down everything wrong.
3. Annotate that same clip. Compare with Muwafag's version and discuss **every**
   disagreement — that conversation becomes our annotation standard, so write it down.
4. Report: how long did it take, and which step was slowest? That's our baseline.
5. **In parallel, and this is the urgent one: outreach.** We need **six fresh customer
   interviews completed by Tue 15 September** and none have happened yet. This is the
   critical path for the whole programme phase, and it is largely yours.
   - Read `interviews/README.md` — who we need and why
   - Read `outreach/linkedin-buyer-search.md` — where to find them
   - Use the templates in `outreach/outreach-kit.md` (English and Arabic)
   - Log **everything** in `interviews/tracker.md`
   - Expect 30–40 messages to land 6 interviews. Send them within 48 hours.

   Muwafag runs the interviews. You find the people, send the messages, chase the replies,
   schedule the calls, and take the notes using `interviews/notes/TEMPLATE.md`.

## How we work
- Everything lives in this repository. If it isn't written down here, it doesn't exist —
  sessions are wiped between uses.
- Commit your work. Update `TASKS.md` when something changes.
- **Ask rather than assume.** If an annotation case is ambiguous, don't guess — flag it. An
  inconsistent label is worse than a missing one.
- Record what surprises you. Surprises are where the learning is.
