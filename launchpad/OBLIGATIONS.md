# Mandatory obligations — Phase 1

From the Phase 1 Startup Dashboard. These are the compliance items filtration will check.
Founders lose cuts on missed obligations far more often than on weak strategy — these are
cheap to satisfy and expensive to miss.

## Phase 1 Startup Checklist (official)
1. **Attend live sessions** — masterclasses, workshops, discussions. Interact and ask questions.
2. **Weekly mentor sync** — 1:1 with your *assigned* mentor. **Only the assigned mentor.
   One meeting per week.** Book using your **Startup ID**. Show up on time.
3. **Submit Assignments 1 & 2** — after the dedicated sessions, not before.
4. **Submit the weekly feedback form** — mandatory.
5. **Get to know the cohort** — icebreaker, Startup Directory, WhatsApp.

## Note on the mentor sync
This is the single most underrated asset in the program and it is *mandatory anyway*.
The assigned mentor is a warm, obligated, weekly channel to the ecosystem. Come to each
sync with one specific ask, not a status update. For Muwafag the standing asks are:
introductions to academies (Assignment #1's binding constraint), the Ministry of Sport
footage-licensing path, and a route to computer-vision expertise (PDD Ask #1).
