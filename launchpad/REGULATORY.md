# Saudi sport regulatory landscape — the real trigger
Source: Lizzie Fluke, *Saudi–MENA Sport Environments*, Edition 5 (published newsletter,
shared by the author). Captured 12 Sep 2026.

**This replaces the hearsay in `BUSINESS_MODEL.md`.** The governance trigger is real, it is
statutory, and it is dated.

---

## 1. The Sports Law — Royal Decree No. M/121 ⭐
| | |
|---|---|
| **Enacted** | 1 December 2025 |
| **Effective** | 11 June 2026 |
| **Transition period ends** | **11 June 2027** |
| **Requirement** | Affected sports entities and individuals must regularise their status and complete registration in the **National Sports Registry** |
| **If not registered** | May not be allowed to **operate, access funding, or participate in official competitions** |
| **Enforcement** | Ministry inspection frameworks + the **Violations Adjudication Committee** |
| **Penalties** | Up to **SAR 5 million**, plus immediate licence suspension or cancellation |

**This is the "why now."** Not a vague expectation that clubs should modernise — a dated
statutory deadline with existential consequences for non-compliance, roughly nine months out.

Darrena's ICP masterclass: *triggered buyers close 3–5× faster and churn far less.* Every
club and academy in the Kingdom is inside this transition window right now.

## 2. Club accreditation is tied to funding
> *"to receive licenses and government funding, clubs must connect their internal coaching
> standards and administrative processes with national benchmarks."*

This is the funding-compliance link we had only as hearsay from Muwafag. It is real, and it
runs through accreditation rather than through a direct "buy analytics software" instruction.

**Calibration:** the mandate is about **governance capability, registration and accreditation**
— not specifically about buying tactical analysis tools. Our earlier framing ("the Ministry
requires academies to implement advanced analytics") was **too literal**. The accurate version
is that data maturity is one dimension of the capability clubs are being pushed up, and
evidence of it supports accreditation and funding.

## 3. ⚠ PDPL — a compliance gap in OUR product
Saudi's **Personal Data Protection Law** governs athlete data. The article is explicit that a
mature system requires:
- **Data minimisation and purpose limitation** — collect only what a specific purpose needs
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and audit trails** — audited, role-specific access tiers
- **Culturally aware, bilingual consent workflows** that define data responsibilities across
  federations, clubs, athletes and providers
- **Explicit protection of minors in youth academy systems**

**Playbook-IQ processes tracking data on identifiable young players from video.** PDPL has not
been considered once in our planning. For a product whose beachhead is youth academies, and
whose buyers are being pushed into a compliance regime, **this is both a risk and an
opportunity**:
- *Risk:* a buyer's governance function will ask, and we have no answer.
- *Opportunity:* being PDPL-aware by design, with minor consent handled properly, is a
  differentiator no international competitor will have bothered with.

**Action: this belongs in Assignment #2's MVP roadmap and in PDD Ask #2 (regulatory support).**

## 4. The Federation Capability Maturity Model (FCMM©) — Lizzie's framework
Five levels, diagnosing capability across governance, operations, workforce, **data maturity**,
and medical structures.

| Level | Data maturity |
|---|---|
| 0 | Participation foundation. Data minimal. |
| 1–2 | **Level 2: data collection intermittent and irregular, rarely used to guide strategy. Workforce lacks data literacy. Records spread across unlinked spreadsheets or paper logs.** |
| 3 | Data supports selection, participation tracking, early performance planning. Governance shifts to pathway stewardship. |
| 4 | Longitudinal data informs planning and injury reduction. Workforce expands across coaching, medical, **analysis**, psychology. Club accreditation and coach licensing implemented. |
| 5 | Integrated pathway ecosystem — analysis operating as a coordinated multidisciplinary function centred on the athlete. |

### Why this matters to us
**Our customers are Level 2. Our product is a Level 2 → 3/4 accelerator on the data dimension.**

That sentence, in her own model, is the most precise description of Playbook-IQ's role anyone
has produced — including us. Level 2 is *exactly* Alaa: intermittent data, no literacy,
records in unlinked files.

Her line: *"Digitalisation and AI can accelerate progress at any stage, but only when
underpinned by governance clarity, operational integration, workforce capability, and
consistent implementation."* — a warning we should take seriously, and echo back.

## 5. The LDI — Leaders Development Institute (معهد إعداد القادة)
The Ministry of Sport's capability-building arm. Responsible for:
- Developing the national sport workforce
- Setting **competency frameworks for accreditation and licensing**
- Improving governance capability and auditing institutional performance
- **Training sports executives and technical directors to govern via data-driven workflows**
- *"Embeds institutional performance metrics, digital competency, and modern sports science
  analytics directly into its core leadership and administrative curricula"*

**Alaa already offered to introduce us to معهد إعداد القادة.** That referral now looks far more
valuable than it did — the LDI **trains our buyers**, sets the standards they are accredited
against, and is explicitly tasked with building data literacy in technical directors.

**Potential channel, not just a contact.** Chase this.

## 6. The delivery-layer gap
SOPC has deployed **SAP** (S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Analytics Cloud) as a unified
digital core. Her assessment: it works as a **corporate and financial platform**, delivering
Level 3–4 governance and financial clarity — but **it is not yet a sporting delivery layer**,
and does not reach Level 5.

The gap she names is the **delivery spine**: integration with localised Athlete Management
Systems and medical records, unifying pathway progression and readiness workflows on the ground.

**Playbook-IQ is a delivery-layer component on the technical/tactical dimension.** That is a
sophisticated way to position for an institutional audience, and it uses their vocabulary.

That is **Alaa, described from the system level.** "Time, time, time" and "cognitive load from

## What this changes
1. **The trigger is real, statutory and dated** — 11 June 2027. Use it, precisely.
2. **Stop overclaiming it.** The law mandates registration and accreditation, not analytics
   purchases. Data maturity supports accreditation; it is not itself the requirement.
3. **PDPL must enter the roadmap.** Youth athlete data, consent, minors, RBAC.
4. **Adopt FCMM language with institutional buyers** — "Level 2 to Level 4 on data maturity."
5. **The LDI is a potential channel**, and we already have an offered introduction.
6. **Verify independently.** This is one expert's published account. Confirm M/121's dates and
   requirements from a primary source before putting them in a deck or an investor conversation.
